About

I am a data science and analytics leader with over a decade of experience applying statistical modeling, machine learning, and artificial intelligence to complex challenges in higher education. My work focuses on building analytical capabilities that support strategic planning, improve institutional effectiveness, and enable more informed decision-making.

Throughout my career, I have led initiatives spanning enrollment management, admissions strategy, student success, financial planning, and university advancement. These efforts have included developing predictive models, enrollment and tuition revenue forecasting systems, scholarship optimization frameworks, and interactive decision-support tools. I am particularly interested in connecting advanced analytical methods with practical applications that help institutions navigate complex decisions and plan for the future.

I currently serve as Director, Enrollment Analytics, Planning, and AI at Johns Hopkins University. Previously, I held strategic analytics and data science roles at Princeton University and New Jersey Institute of Technology. At Princeton, I worked on strategic analytics, enterprise data infrastructure, and applied AI. I also developed conversational analytics tools that enabled users to interact with institutional data through natural language. At NJIT, I led data science initiatives and collaborated with academic and administrative leadership to translate institutional priorities into scalable analytical solutions.

I hold a Ph.D. in Psychometrics and an M.S. in Statistics from Rutgers University. My academic background provides a foundation in statistical reasoning, measurement, and rigorous model evaluation, which continues to inform my approach to applied data science. I value analytical approaches that are not only technically sound, but also transparent, interpretable, and useful to decision-makers.

Alongside my professional work, I teach Global Data Analytics in the Master of Public Informatics (MPI) program at Rutgers University's Bloustein School of Planning and Public Policy. My teaching focuses on applying data analytics and statistical methods to real-world policy challenges with an emphasis on developing practical analytical skills and evidence-based decision-making.

This website brings together my professional work, scholarship, and teaching and mentorship activities.
